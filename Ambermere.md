## Overview
Ambermere is a land of mystery. Home to only a single town, the infertile mountainous landscape allows for little life. Filled mainly with jagged mountain peaks, many an explorer have been turned away by the cruel terrain. Stories of monsters and vampires living atop the mountains are what most know of Ambermere. There seems to be an ever-present layer of fog and cloud. 
## Flag
Much like the Icebound Desert, the flag was designed for Ambermere by others. The flag shows the iconic Bloodstone, a highly sought-after mineral found in the [[Bloodgut Mine]]

## Politics

Nonexistent. There are no governing powers and no laws.
## Resources & Trade

The odd expedition goes into Ambermere looking for Bloodstone, but nobody in Ambermere ever sees any profit from this.
